# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Metis in Social Science is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and
harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, ability, appearance, beliefs, or personal circumstances.

We expect every participant to help create a constructive environment for
researchers, users, contributors, and maintainers.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that supports this community include:

- treating people with empathy, patience, and respect;
- welcoming different viewpoints and research traditions;
- giving specific, constructive feedback and receiving it in good faith;
- acknowledging mistakes, repairing harm where possible, and learning from it;
- protecting private research material, credentials, and personal information;
- focusing disagreements on evidence, implementation, and user impact rather
  than on the person presenting an idea.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory, insulting, sexualized, or demeaning language or imagery;
- personal or political attacks unrelated to the work;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- knowingly sharing credentials, private research data, or identifying evidence;
- conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests,
reviews, community spaces, and public situations where a participant is
officially representing the project.

## Reporting

Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue. Submit a private report through
the repository's
[GitHub Security Advisory form](https://github.com/TZUKWAN/metis-in-social-science/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Code of Conduct`.

Include the relevant dates, locations, participants, and evidence necessary to
understand the report. Remove unrelated credentials, private documents, research
data, and personally identifying information.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as reasonably possible,
protect the safety and privacy of reporters, investigate fairly, and avoid
conflicts of interest. A maintainer named in a report must not participate in
its review or enforcement decision.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit content, close or lock discussions, reject
contributions, restrict participation, or impose temporary or permanent bans
when necessary to protect the community. Responses will be proportionate to the
severity, impact, context, and recurrence of the behavior. When appropriate,
maintainers will explain the decision privately to the affected participant.

Enforcement may progress through these levels:

1. A private correction explaining the impact and expected behavior.
2. A formal warning with clear conditions for continued participation.
3. A temporary restriction from community interaction.
4. A permanent ban for severe abuse or a sustained pattern of violations.

Retaliation against a reporter, witness, or person participating in an
investigation is itself a violation of this Code of Conduct.
